Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the implementation process take? ▼

Typical implementation takes 4-8 weeks depending on your institution size and existing systems. Our team will provide a customized timeline during the initial consultation.

Does ACRM integrate with our existing systems? ▼

Yes! ACRM integrates seamlessly with most popular campus ERP systems, learning management systems, and student information systems.

What kind of training do you provide? ▼

We offer comprehensive training including staff workshops, administrator certification, end-user guides, and ongoing support.

Is ACRM cloud-based or on-premise? ▼

ACRM is available both as a cloud-based SaaS solution and as an on-premise deployment, depending on your institution requirements.
